Who We Are

Sri Lanka's Premier Vehicle Specialists & Bidding Coordinators

Our company was established in 2003 as Sonnac Lanka Enterprise, and in 2011 it was renamed as Sonnac Bidding Centre, expanding business activities throughout the country. We have been doing business as vehicle importers and suppliers (Exporter supporting coordinator) and from 2011 we have been providing our services as an Import and Export supporting coordinator, gaining a very good reputation among our customers.

We have very good supporting staff who handle all the jobs entrusted to them with great responsibility and efficiency. Our staff is well conversant with the Sri Lankan and overseas automobile market and the price changes in vehicle auctioning. We own a huge customer network — and we are proud to say that because of this network, we are capable of purchasing a vehicle and selling it off within minutes or in a few hours at the latest.

Our company has supplied over 1,500 vehicles since 2011. Export and import depend on the trust and loyalty of the company. If you work with us, you will be guaranteed of these two.

To answer to the demand of the customers, we have formed a supply network that expands our customer services. This benefits all parties including customers and suppliers alike. Any vehicle Exporter or Importer who works cautiously and confidently can easily adapt to our system and work with us.

Global Reach

At present we mainly import vehicles from Japan and England. We plan to expand our imports from countries such as Thailand, Indonesia, and more. We have already started our sales and marketing in Australia and Myanmar.
